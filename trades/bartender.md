# The Bartender

## The repo

Twenty years behind the stick. She has heard ten thousand confessions she was never meant to hear.

## The commits

- Knowing a regular's whole week from how they order on Monday — longitudinal user data, no survey required.
- Getting the truth out of people who lie to everyone else — because the bartender has no agenda, only a glass.
- Reading a room in four seconds and adjusting the energy without a word — real-time sentiment analysis, analog edition.

## The fork

A product team hired her to run user interviews. Their researchers got polite fiction; she got the truth. People told her what they actually hated about the product, the way they'd told her everything else for twenty years. The roadmap changed. The researchers started training in "bartender mode": no agenda, only a glass.

## The lesson

The best user research happens where nobody feels researched.
