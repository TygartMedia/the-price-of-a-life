# The Repo Man

## The repo

He took people's cars for a living. Nine years. Nobody ever thanked him.

## The commits

- Talking a furious man out of his truck at 2 AM without anyone getting hurt — de-escalation under genuine threat, practiced weekly.
- Reading in thirty seconds whether a situation would go verbal, physical, or quiet — threat assessment with no second chances.
- The jobs that went wrong and the ones he walked away from — knowing the difference between a situation you can cool and one you can't.

## The fork

A security consultancy brought him in to train de-escalation. Their curriculum was theory; his was nine years of driveways at 2 AM. He taught their people the thirty-second read, the voice that lowers instead of raises, and when to walk away. Incident reports dropped. Nobody calms a furious man like someone who's taken his truck and lived.

## The lesson

Conflict resolution is a practiced skill, and the practice field was a driveway.
